Quick Version: Bob and Polly Ann Powers Dressing and Gravy

Same dressing, assembled and baked. Buy the broth and the giblets. Bake the cornbread, or buy it.

Time What to skip Ingredients Pans Order Steps Full recipe

Time

Buy the cornbreadabout 1 hour 15 minutes
Bake the cornbreadabout 2 hours
Hands-on work, either wayabout 40 minutes

The full recipe takes 3–3½ hours because the turkey stock simmers for about 2 hours. This version buys that broth, so the dressing bake is the long step.

What to skip

Buy a carton of turkey broth. That removes the turkey legs, the browning, and the 1½–2 hour simmer.

Buy a pack of turkey giblets, and the neck if it is sold with them. You do not need a whole turkey. Simmer the pack while you do the other work.

Use butter for the gravy in place of fat from a roast. If a turkey is already roasting, use ¼ cup of its drippings instead.

Buy shredded cheddar, and pre-cut onion, celery, and garlic if the store has them.

Cornbread is a choice. Bake it, about 30 minutes in the oven, or buy 4 cups already made and crumble it.

Ingredients

Check these off at the store. Pick one cornbread path.

Always buy

Cornbread, buy or bake

Buy 4 cups crumbled cornbread from the bakery. Plain or lightly sweet. Skip the bake ingredients.

Bake 1 cup cornmeal, 1 cup buttermilk, 1 large egg, ¼ cup vegetable oil, 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda, ½ tsp salt. The bake is 30 minutes.

Pots and pans

Also set out a large mixing bowl, a whisk, a knife, and a board. No stockpot and no roasting pan.

Steps

  1. Bake Heat the oven to 400°F. Stir the cornmeal, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Mix the buttermilk, 1 egg, and ¼ cup oil in another bowl. Stir the wet into the dry just until combined. Pour it into a greased 8-inch dish or skillet.about 10 minutes

  2. Bake Bake until the cornbread is golden and a toothpick comes out clean. Cool it until you can handle it, then crumble 4 cups.30 minutes to bake, about 15 minutes to cool

  3. Buy Skip the two steps above. Crumble the bought cornbread until you have 4 cups. Heat the oven to 350°F.about 5 minutes

  4. Put the giblets and the neck in a small saucepan with water to cover. Simmer until they are cooked through. Drain and chop them fine. Start this when the oven goes on so it finishes during the other work.25–30 minutes, mostly hands-off

  5. Heat about 1 tablespoon of oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion, celery, and garlic until soft.5–7 minutes

  6. Tear the buns into pieces about the size of the cornbread crumbs. Toast them on a baking sheet at 350°F until lightly golden and dry. Toss once. If the buns are already stale and dry, 5 minutes is enough.5–15 minutes

  7. In a large bowl, mix the cornbread, toasted buns, cooked vegetables, ½ tsp black pepper, and salt. Carton broth is often salty, so taste before you add much salt. A pinch of red pepper flakes is optional.about 5 minutes

  8. Stir in one stick of melted butter, 6 beaten eggs, the shredded cheddar, and the sausage or cooked turkey if you are using it. Add turkey broth a little at a time until the mixture is moist but not soggy. Use 1–2 cups.about 10 minutes

  9. Spread it in a greased 9×13 baking dish. Bake at 350°F until the top is golden and crisp.30–40 minutes

  10. While the dressing bakes, make the gravy in the giblet saucepan. Melt ¼ cup butter over medium heat. Whisk in ¼ cup flour until the roux is golden.2–3 minutes

  11. Whisk in about 2 cups of turkey broth, a little at a time. Add the chopped giblets and neck. Simmer until the gravy coats a spoon. Salt and pepper to taste. If you have real roast drippings, use ¼ cup of that fat instead of the butter.5–10 minutes

  12. Serve the dressing warm, with the gravy over it, and Ocean Spray cranberry sauce on the table.
