Bob and Polly Ann Powers Dressing and Gravy

Circa 2024, as recalled by Pam Sheridan and Mack Powers.

Time Ingredients Pans Order Steps

Time

If the turkey broth is already madeabout 2 hours
Gravy, mostly while the turkey roastsabout 45 minutes
Hands-on work for the long dayabout 1 hour 20 minutes

Step times overlap. The stock simmers while the cornbread and dressing are made. The clock above is start to finish, not the sum of every step.

Ingredients

Check these off at the store. Onions, celery, garlic, and eggs are totaled so the dressing and the stock are not bought twice.

To buy

At home: 64 oz filtered water for the stock.

As the card groups them

Cornbread. 1 cup cornmeal, 1 cup buttermilk, 1 large egg, ¼ cup vegetable oil, 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda, ½ tsp salt.

Dressing. 4 cups crumbled cornbread, 8 hamburger buns torn into pieces, 1 medium onion chopped, 2–3 stalks celery chopped, 2–3 cloves garlic minced, 1–2 cups turkey broth, ½ tsp black pepper, salt to taste, a pinch of red pepper flakes (optional), 6 large eggs beaten, 16 oz medium cheddar grated, 1 stick butter melted, 1 cup cooked sausage or chopped cooked turkey (optional).

Stock. 2–3 turkey legs, 2 onions quartered, 4 celery stalks chopped, 4 cloves garlic smashed, 2 bay leaves, 32 oz chicken broth, 64 oz filtered water, salt and pepper to taste.

Gravy. Turkey drippings, ¼ cup of the fat from those drippings, ¼ cup flour, turkey broth, the giblets, and the neck. Salt, pepper, a little butter, and fresh herbs to finish.

Pots and pans

Also set out a fine-mesh sieve, a large mixing bowl, two smaller bowls, a whisk, a measuring cup, a grater, a knife, and a board.

Order of work

This is the same-day path. The oven starts at 400°F for the legs and the cornbread, then drops to 350°F for the buns and the dressing.

  • 0:00Heat the oven to 400°F. Season the turkey legs. Chop the stock vegetables.
  • 0:15Legs go in the oven.
  • 0:50Legs into the stockpot with the vegetables, bay leaves, chicken broth, and water. Bring it to a boil.
  • 1:05Stock simmers on low. Mix the cornbread and put it in the oven, still at 400°F.
  • 1:30Cornbread out to cool. Set the oven to 350°F. Cook the dressing vegetables. Tear the buns. Grate the cheese, beat the eggs, and melt the butter.
  • 1:50Toast the buns.
  • 2:05Crumble the cornbread and mix the dressing. Ladle 1–2 cups of broth from the simmering stock.
  • 2:20Dressing goes into the oven.
  • 2:55Dressing comes out. The stock has simmered about 1¾ hours. Give it the rest of the 2 hours if you want, then strain it.

If the broth is already made, start at the cornbread. Mix, bake, and cool it, toast the buns, mix the dressing, and bake. That path is about 2 hours.

Make the gravy when the roasted turkey comes out. Simmer the giblets and neck during the roast so they are chopped and waiting.

Steps

Stock

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Season 2–3 turkey legs with salt and pepper. Set them on a sheet pan or in a roasting pan.about 15 minutes, including the oven

  2. Roast the legs until they are browned.30–40 minutes

  3. While they roast, quarter 2 onions, chop 4 celery stalks, and smash 4 garlic cloves.about 10 minutes, during the roast

  4. Put the browned legs in a large stockpot with the onions, celery, garlic, and 2 bay leaves. Add 32 oz chicken broth and 64 oz filtered water. The legs should be covered. Add a little water if they are not. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat.about 15 minutes

  5. Turn the heat to low. Simmer, and skim off the foam that rises.1½–2 hours, mostly hands-off

  6. When you mix the dressing, ladle out 1–2 cups of this broth. Leave the rest simmering.

  7. Take out the legs. Strain the stock through a fine-mesh sieve into a large bowl. Discard the vegetables and the bay leaves. Season with salt and pepper. Cool what you will not use today, then refrigerate or freeze it.about 15 minutes, plus cooling

Cornbread

Start this when the legs come out of the oven. The oven is already at 400°F.

  1. In one bowl, stir the cornmeal, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. In another bowl, mix the buttermilk, 1 egg, and ¼ cup vegetable oil. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir just until combined.about 8 minutes

  2. Pour the batter into a greased 8-inch square baking dish or skillet. Bake until it is golden and a toothpick comes out clean.20–25 minutes

  3. Cool the cornbread, then crumble it. The dressing wants 4 cups.about 20 minutes to cool, then 5 minutes to crumble

Dressing

  1. Heat about 1 tablespoon of oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the chopped onion, 2–3 chopped celery stalks, and 2–3 minced garlic cloves. Cook until they are soft.5–7 minutes

  2. Tear the 8 hamburger buns into pieces about the size of the cornbread crumbs. Spread them on a baking sheet. Toast at 350°F until they are lightly golden and dry. Toss them halfway through.10–15 minutes

  3. In a large bowl, mix the crumbled cornbread, toasted buns, cooked vegetables, ½ tsp black pepper, salt, and a pinch of red pepper flakes if you want a little heat.about 5 minutes

  4. Stir in the melted butter, the 6 beaten eggs, the grated cheddar, and the sausage or cooked turkey if you are using it. Add turkey broth a little at a time until the mixture is moist but not soggy. The card calls for 1–2 cups.about 10 minutes

  5. Spread the mixture in a greased 9×13 baking dish. Bake at 350°F until the top is golden and crisp.30–40 minutes

Gravy

This uses the drippings from a roasted turkey. Simmer the giblets while that turkey roasts.

  1. Rinse the giblets and the neck. Put them in a small saucepan with enough water to cover. Simmer until they are cooked through. Drain, chop them fine, and set them aside.about 30 minutes

  2. Pour the turkey drippings into a measuring cup. Let them sit so the fat rises. Skim off ¼ cup of fat and put it back in the saucepan. Discard the rest of the fat, or save it for something else.about 5 minutes

  3. Set the saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in ¼ cup flour and keep whisking until the roux is golden brown.2–3 minutes

  4. Whisk in turkey broth a little at a time so it stays smooth. Add the chopped giblets and neck. Bring it to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer until it thickens. Start with about 2 cups of broth. The card does not name an amount, so stop when it coats a spoon.5–10 minutes

  5. Taste it. Add salt and pepper. A little butter, or fresh herbs, is optional. Strain it through a fine-mesh sieve if you want it smooth.about 3 minutes

Serve

Serve the dressing warm, with turkey gravy poured over it. Put a can of Ocean Spray cranberry sauce on the table.
